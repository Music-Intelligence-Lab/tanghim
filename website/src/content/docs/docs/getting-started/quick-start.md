---
title: Quick Start
description: Load a maqām and start playing in five steps.
---

1. **Load Tanghīm** on an instrument track in your DAW (it appears under Instruments or Tools).
2. **Choose a tuning system** from the dropdown at the top left (e.g. "Ibn Sīnā (1037) 7-Fret Oud 17-Tone").
3. **Choose a starting note name** from the dropdown next to it (e.g. "yegāh").
4. **Select a maqam** from the maqam selector dropdown.
5. **Play your MIDI keyboard.** Your instrument will receive the tuning via one of three methods depending on its capabilities.

For details on tuning delivery, see [Tuning Methods](/docs/tuning/methods). For Ableton Live-specific setup, see [Ableton Live](/docs/ableton).
